

**Test Cases:**

* **Normal case:**
    * Month: 2
    * Year: 2023
    * Expected output: 28

* **Leap year case:**
    * Month: 2
    * Year: 2024
    * Expected output: 29

* **Corner case: invalid month:**
    * Month: 13
    * Year: 2023
    * Expected output: Invalid month

* **Corner case: invalid year:**
    * Month: 2
    * Year: 1900
    * Expected output: Invalid year
