

**Test Cases:**

**Corner Cases:**

* Two binary numbers of different lengths
* Two binary numbers with leading zeros
* Two binary numbers with trailing zeros

**Normal Cases:**

* Two binary numbers of the same length
* Two binary numbers with no leading or trailing zeros
* Two binary numbers with a mix of leading and trailing zeros

**Examples:**

* **Input:** 10, 11
* **Output:** 101

* **Input:** 100, 111
* **Output:** 1011

* **Input:** 1010, 1111
* **Output:** 11001

* **Input:** 111011, 100110
* **Output:** 1011101
