

**Test Cases:**

* **Valid Inputs:**
    * a
    * e
    * i
    * o
    * u
    * A
    * E
    * I
    * O
    * U
* **Invalid Inputs:**
    * 1 (not a letter)
    * ab (string of length > 1)
    * # (not a letter)
    *  (empty string)
* **Corner Cases:**
    * aA (mixed case letter)
    * Z (capital letter)
