

**Test Cases:**

**Normal Cases:**

* Manager with base salary of 5000.00 and bonus of 1000.00 should have a salary of 6000.00.
* Programmer with base salary of 4000.00 and overtime pay of 500.00 should have a salary of 4500.00.

**Corner Cases:**

* Manager with negative base salary should throw an exception.
* Programmer with negative base salary or overtime pay should throw an exception.
* Manager with negative bonus should throw an exception.
* Programmer with negative overtime pay should throw an exception.
