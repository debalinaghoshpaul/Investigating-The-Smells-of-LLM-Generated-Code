

**Test Cases**

**Normal Cases**

* **Test Case 1:**
    * Input: Today's date: 2023-03-08
    * Expected output:
        * One year ago: 2022-03-08
        * Is one year ago before today? true
        * One year from now: 2024-03-08
        * Is one year from now after today? true
* **Test Case 2:**
    * Input: Today's date: 2020-02-29 (leap year)
    * Expected output:
        * One year ago: 2019-02-28
        * Is one year ago before today? true
        * One year from now: 2021-02-28
        * Is one year from now after today? true

**Corner Cases**

* **Test Case 3:**
    * Input: Today's date: 2020-02-29 (leap year)
    * Expected output:
        * One year ago: 2019-02-28
        * Is one year ago before today? true
        * One year from now: 2021-03-01 (not a leap year)
        * Is one year from now after today? true
* **Test Case 4:**
    * Input: Today's date: 2020-03-01
    * Expected output:
        * One year ago: 2019-03-01
        * Is one year ago before today? true
        * One year from now: 2021-03-01
        * Is one year from now after today? true
