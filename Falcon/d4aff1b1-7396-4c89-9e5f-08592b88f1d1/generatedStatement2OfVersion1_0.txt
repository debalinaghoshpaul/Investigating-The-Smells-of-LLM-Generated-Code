

**Test cases:**

* **Valid cases:**
    * Deposit and withdraw within the withdrawal limit for a SavingsAccount.
    * Deposit and withdraw within the overdraft limit for a CheckingAccount.
* **Corner cases:**
    * Withdraw an amount of 0 from both SavingsAccount and CheckingAccount.
    * Create SavingsAccount and CheckingAccount objects with negative initial balances.
* **Invalid cases:**
    * Withdraw an amount greater than the withdrawal limit for a SavingsAccount.
    * Withdraw an amount greater than the overdraft limit for a CheckingAccount.
