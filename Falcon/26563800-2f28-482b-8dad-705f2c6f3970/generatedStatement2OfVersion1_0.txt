

**Test Cases:**

* **Normal Cases:**
    * Input: 5
    * Output: 101
    * Input: 10
    * Output: 1010
    * Input: 15
    * Output: 1111

* **Edge Cases:**
    * Input: 0
    * Output: 0
    * Input: 1
    * Output: 1
    * Input: -5
    * Output: Error (negative numbers not supported)
    * Input: 1000000000
    * Output: Error (number too large)

* **Corner Cases:**
    * Input: Integer.MAX_VALUE
    * Output: Error (number too large)
    * Input: Integer.MIN_VALUE
    * Output: Error (negative numbers not supported)
