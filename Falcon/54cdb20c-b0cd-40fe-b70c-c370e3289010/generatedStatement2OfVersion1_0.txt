

**Test Cases:**

* **Corner Cases:**
    * Test with a date that is very close to the beginning or end of an epoch.
    * Test with a date that has a fractional second component.
    * Test with a date that is in a different time zone.
* **Normal Cases:**
    * Test with a date that is in the middle of an epoch.
    * Test with a date that has no fractional second component.
    * Test with a date that is in the same time zone as the computer running the program.
