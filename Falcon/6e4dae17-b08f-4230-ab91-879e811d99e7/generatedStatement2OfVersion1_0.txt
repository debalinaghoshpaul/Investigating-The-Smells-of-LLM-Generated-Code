

**Test Cases:**

**Normal Cases:**

* Input: `[1, 2, 3, 4, 5]`
* Expected Output:
    * Sum of squares of odd numbers: 25
    * Sum of squares of even numbers: 30

* Input: `[2, 4, 6, 8, 10]`
* Expected Output:
    * Sum of squares of odd numbers: 0
    * Sum of squares of even numbers: 200

**Corner Cases:**

* Input: `[]` (empty list)
* Expected Output:
    * Sum of squares of odd numbers: 0
    * Sum of squares of even numbers: 0

* Input: `[0]`
* Expected Output:
    * Sum of squares of odd numbers: 0
    * Sum of squares of even numbers: 0

* Input: `[1, 3, 5, 7]` (only odd numbers)
* Expected Output:
    * Sum of squares of odd numbers: 25
    * Sum of squares of even numbers: 0

* Input: `[2, 4, 6, 8]` (only even numbers)
* Expected Output:
    * Sum of squares of odd numbers: 0
    * Sum of squares of even numbers: 100
