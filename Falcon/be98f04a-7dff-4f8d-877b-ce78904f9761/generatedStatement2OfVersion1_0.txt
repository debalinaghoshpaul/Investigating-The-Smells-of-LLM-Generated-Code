

**Test Cases:**

**Valid Cases:**

* Create a rectangle with width 5.0 and height 10.0, and draw it.
* Create a circle with radius 3.0, and draw it.
* Resize the rectangle by a factor of 1.5, and draw it again.
* Resize the circle by a factor of 2.0, and draw it again.

**Corner Cases:**

* Create a rectangle with width 0.0 and height 0.0 (will not draw anything).
* Create a circle with radius 0.0 (will not draw anything).
* Resize a rectangle or circle by a factor of 0.0 (will not change the shape's size).
* Resize a rectangle or circle with negative factor (will result in an error).
