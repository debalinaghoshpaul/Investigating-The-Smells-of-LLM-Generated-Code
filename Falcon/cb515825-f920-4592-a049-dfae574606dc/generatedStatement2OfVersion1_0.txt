

**Test Cases:**

* **Positive integers:**
    * 12345 -> 1
    * 67890 -> 6
    * 98765 -> 9
* **Negative integers:**
    * -12345 -> 1
    * -67890 -> 6
    * -98765 -> 9
* **Zero:**
    * 0 -> 0
* **Corner cases:**
    * Integer.MAX_VALUE -> 2 (the first digit of the maximum integer value)
    * Integer.MIN_VALUE -> -2 (the first digit of the minimum integer value)
