

**Test Cases:**

**Corner Cases:**

* **Input:** firstInteger = 0, secondInteger = 0
* **Expected Output:** 1

**Normal Cases:**

* **Input:** firstInteger = 123, secondInteger = 456
* **Expected Output:** 3

* **Input:** firstInteger = -123, secondInteger = 456
* **Expected Output:** 3

* **Input:** firstInteger = 12345, secondInteger = 67890
* **Expected Output:** 5

**Negative Cases:**

* **Input:** firstInteger = -12345, secondInteger = -67890
* **Expected Output:** 5 (Note that the negative sign is ignored when computing the sum.)
