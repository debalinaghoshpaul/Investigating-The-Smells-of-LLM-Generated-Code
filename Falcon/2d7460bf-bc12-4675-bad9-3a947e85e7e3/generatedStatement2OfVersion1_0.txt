

**Test Cases:**

**Normal Cases:**

* Create an Eagle object and call its fly() and makeSound() methods.
* Create a Hawk object and call its fly() and makeSound() methods.

**Corner Cases:**

* Create a Bird object without specifying the subclass. Attempt to call its fly() and makeSound() methods.
* Create an Eagle object and attempt to call its makeSound() method without calling its fly() method first.
* Create a Hawk object and attempt to call its fly() method without calling its makeSound() method first.
