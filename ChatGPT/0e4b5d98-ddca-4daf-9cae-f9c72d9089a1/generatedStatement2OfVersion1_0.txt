

**Test Cases:**

**Valid Cases:**

* Add an account with valid account number, account holder name, and initial balance.
* Remove an account with a valid account number.
* Deposit a valid amount of money into an account.
* Withdraw a valid amount of money from an account.
* Check the balance of an account.

**Corner Cases:**

* Add an account with an invalid account number (e.g., empty string, null).
* Add an account with an invalid account holder name (e.g., empty string, null).
* Add an account with a negative initial balance.
* Remove an account with an invalid account number (e.g., empty string, null).
* Deposit a negative amount of money into an account.
* Withdraw an amount that exceeds the account balance.
* Withdraw money from an account with an invalid account number (e.g., empty string, null).
