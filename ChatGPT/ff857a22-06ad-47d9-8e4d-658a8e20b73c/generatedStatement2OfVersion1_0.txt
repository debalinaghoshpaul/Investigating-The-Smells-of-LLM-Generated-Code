

**Test cases:**

* **Negative test case:**
    * Input: P(1, 1), Q(3, 2), R(2, 2), S(4, 3)

* **Corner cases:**
    * Input: P(1, 1), Q(1, 2), R(2, 2), S(2, 1)

    * Input: P(1, 1), Q(1, 2), R(2, 1), S(2, 2)
