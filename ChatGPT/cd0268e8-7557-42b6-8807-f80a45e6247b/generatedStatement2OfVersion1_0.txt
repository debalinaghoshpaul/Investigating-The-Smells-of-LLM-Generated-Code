

**Test Cases:**

**Positive Test Cases:**

* Depositing and withdrawing money from both types of accounts
* Calculating interest for the savings account
* Viewing balances for both types of accounts

**Negative Test Cases:**

* Withdrawing an amount greater than the balance (should not be allowed)
* Withdrawing an amount greater than the balance plus the overdraft limit (for current account, should not be allowed)
* Depositing a negative amount (should not be allowed)
* Calculating interest for the current account (should be 0)

**Corner Cases:**

* Withdrawing an amount equal to the balance (should reduce the balance to 0)
* Withdrawing an amount equal to the balance plus the overdraft limit (for current account, should reduce the balance and overdraft limit to 0)
* Depositing an amount of 0 (should not change the balance)
* Creating an account with a negative balance (should throw an exception)
