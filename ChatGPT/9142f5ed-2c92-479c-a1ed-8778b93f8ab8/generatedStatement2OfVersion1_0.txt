

**Test Cases:**

* **Normal case:**
    * Input: dividend = 625, divisor = 25
    * Output: Result: 25.0
* **Corner cases:**
    * **Dividend is 0:**
        * Input: dividend = 0, divisor = 25
        * Output: Result: 0.0
    * **Divisor is 1:**
        * Input: dividend = 625, divisor = 1
        * Output: Result: 625.0
    * **Divisor is negative:**
        * Input: dividend = 625, divisor = -25
        * Output: Result: -25.0
    * **Dividend is negative:**
        * Input: dividend = -625, divisor = 25
        * Output: Result: -25.0
    * **Both dividend and divisor are negative:**
        * Input: dividend = -625, divisor = -25
        * Output: Result: 25.0
