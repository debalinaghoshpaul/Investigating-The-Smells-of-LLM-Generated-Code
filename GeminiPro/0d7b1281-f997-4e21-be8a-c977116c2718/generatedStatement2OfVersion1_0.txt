

**Test Cases:**

* **Normal case:**
    * Input: Current time
    * Output:
        * Current time: Fri Sep 23 15:35:09 EDT 2022
        * Year: 2022
        * Month: 9
        * Day: 23
        * Hour: 15
        * Minute: 35
        * Second: 09
* **Edge cases:**
    * **Year 0:**
        * Input: Date with year 0
        * Output:
            * **Exception:** java.lang.IllegalArgumentException: year 0 out of range 1 to 292247099
    * **Month 0:**
        * Input: Date with month 0
        * Output:
            * **Exception:** java.lang.IllegalArgumentException: month 0 out of range 1 to 12
    * **Day 0:**
        * Input: Date with day 0
        * Output:
            * **Exception:** java.lang.IllegalArgumentException: day 0 out of range 1 to 31
    * **Hour 0:**
        * Input: Date with hour 0
        * Output:
            * Current time: Fri Sep 23 00:00:00 EDT 2022
            * Year: 2022
            * Month: 9
            * Day: 23
            * Hour: 0
            * Minute: 00
            * Second: 00
    * **Minute 0:**
        * Input: Date with minute 0
        * Output:
            * Current time: Fri Sep 23 15:00:00 EDT 2022
            * Year: 2022
            * Month: 9
            * Day: 23
            * Hour: 15
            * Minute: 00
            * Second: 00
    * **Second 0:**
        * Input: Date with second 0
        * Output:
            * Current time: Fri Sep 23 15:35:00 EDT 2022
            * Year: 2022
            * Month: 9
            * Day: 23
            * Hour: 15
            * Minute: 35
            * Second: 00
