

**Test Cases (including corner cases):**

* **Valid Cases:**
    * "Hello123_"
    * "123_abc"
    * "a_b_c_1_2_3"

* **Invalid Cases:**
    * "hello123" (contains lowercase letters)
    * "abc123!" (contains a special character)
    * "" (empty string)
    * null (null input)
    * "123" (contains only numbers)
    * "abc-" (contains a hyphen)
