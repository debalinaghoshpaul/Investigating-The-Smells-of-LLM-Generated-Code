

**Test Cases:**

**Normal Cases:**

* Input: Current month is January
* Output:
    * Month name: January
    * Month number: 1
    * Number of days in the month: 31

* Input: Current month is June
* Output:
    * Month name: June
    * Month number: 6
    * Number of days in the month: 30

**Corner Cases:**

* Input: Current month is February in a leap year
* Output:
    * Month name: February
    * Month number: 2
    * Number of days in the month: 29

* Input: Current month is December in a non-leap year
* Output:
    * Month name: December
    * Month number: 12
    * Number of days in the month: 31
