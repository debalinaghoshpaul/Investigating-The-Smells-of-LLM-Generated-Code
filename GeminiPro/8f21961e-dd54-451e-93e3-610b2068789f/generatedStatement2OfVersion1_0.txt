

### Test Cases:

**Positive Test Cases:**

* `""` (Empty string)
* `"Hello"` (Non-empty string)

**Negative Test Cases:**

* `null` (Null string)

**Corner Cases:**

* `" "` (String with only a space character)
* `"\t"` (String with only a tab character)
* `"\n"` (String with only a newline character)
