

**Test Cases:**

**Corner Cases:**

* **Negative number:** `float number = -123.456789f;`
* **Number with trailing zeroes:** `float number = 123.000000f;`
* **Number less than 1:** `float number = 0.0001f;`

**Regular Cases:**

* **Number with no decimals:** `float number = 123f;`
* **Number with one decimal:** `float number = 123.4f;`
* **Number with more than the specified decimals:** `float number = 123.456789f;`

**Expected Output:**

**Corner Cases:**

* **Negative number:** `Rounded number: -123.46`
* **Number with trailing zeroes:** `Rounded number: 123`
* **Number less than 1:** `Rounded number: 0.00`

**Regular Cases:**

* **Number with no decimals:** `Rounded number: 123`
* **Number with one decimal:** `Rounded number: 123.4`
* **Number with more than the specified decimals:** `Rounded number: 123.46`
