

**Test cases including corner cases:**

1. **Deposit and withdraw a positive amount from a SavingsAccount.**
   - Expected output: The balance should be updated accordingly.

2. **Withdraw more money than the balance from a SavingsAccount.**
   - Expected output: The withdrawal should not be allowed, and the balance should remain unchanged.

3. **Deposit a negative amount from a SavingsAccount.**
   - Expected output: The deposit should not be allowed, and the balance should remain unchanged.

4. **Deposit and withdraw a positive amount from a CurrentAccount.**
   - Expected output: The balance should be updated accordingly.

5. **Withdraw more money than the balance and overdraft limit from a CurrentAccount.**
   - Expected output: The withdrawal should not be allowed, and the balance should remain unchanged.

6. **Deposit a negative amount from a CurrentAccount.**
   - Expected output: The deposit should not be allowed, and the balance should remain unchanged.

7. **Withdraw all the money from a SavingsAccount.**
   - Expected output: The balance should be zero.

8. **Withdraw all the money from a CurrentAccount.**
   - Expected output: The balance should be zero.

9. **Deposit a large amount of money into a SavingsAccount.**
   - Expected output: The balance should be updated accordingly.

10. **Deposit a large amount of money into a CurrentAccount.**
   - Expected output: The balance should be updated accordingly.
