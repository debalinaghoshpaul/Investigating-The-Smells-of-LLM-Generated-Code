

**Test Cases:**

**Corner Cases:**

* **Empty array:** `[]`
* **Array with all elements equal to zero:** `[0, 0, 0, ..., 0]`

**General Cases:**

* **Array with positive and negative elements:** `[1, -2, 2, -1, 3]`
* **Array with all negative elements:** `[-1, -2, -3, -4, -5]`
* **Array with duplicate elements:** `[1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3]`
* **Array with large absolute values:** `[1000000, -1000000, 2000000, -2000000]`
